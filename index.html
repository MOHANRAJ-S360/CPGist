About CPGist

Built for the realities of CPG analytics.

CPGist is an AI-powered analytics concept focused on making consumer packaged goods and FMCG retail data easier to interrogate and communicate. It brings commercial metrics and AI-assisted analysis into a workflow designed for clarity — not black-box answers.

The principle is simple: show what the data supports, explain the reasoning, and state what cannot yet be concluded. Sample figures on this website are illustrative and should not be interpreted as actual market measurements.

Frequently asked questions

Good decisions start with clear expectations.

Who is CPGist designed for?

Brand managers, category teams, sales and revenue-growth professionals, retail analysts and business leaders working with CPG or FMCG performance data.

Does CPGist use real market data in this website preview?

No. Metrics and charts shown in the website preview are illustrative synthetic examples. Actual insights depend on the dataset connected to a CPGist deployment.

Can the AI Analyst answer every business question?

No. Its answer quality depends on the available data, data quality and the evidence required by the question. Unsupported competitor, regional or causal claims should be identified as unavailable rather than invented.

Is the sign-in page connected to authentication?

Account creation and sign-in are handled by the existing CPGist AI workspace using the same Supabase identity system. Choose Launch App or Create account to enter the workspace; a separate marketing-site password database is not used.
